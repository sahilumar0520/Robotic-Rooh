About

Between the lab and the page

I am Umar Nisar, a laboratory professional, the person behind PrimePath Diagnostics, and a writer who finds meaning in the quieter details of life.

My background in medical laboratory science has shaped me in ways that go far beyond professional knowledge. Working in a laboratory teaches you that precision matters, that even the smallest detail can make a difference, and that behind every sample, every investigation and every report is a human being waiting for an answer. It has taught me patience, responsibility, discipline and, above all, respect for the trust people place in diagnostic science. I believe this experience has made me look at life a little differently: more observant, more thoughtful and more conscious of the human stories behind the things we often see as routine.

Beyond the laboratory, I have always been drawn to literature, philosophy and poetry. I am the kind of person who finds companionship in books, meaning in ordinary moments and expression in words that are not always easy to say aloud. My writing leans towards introspection, human emotions, relationships, solitude and the questions we carry within ourselves. Influenced by the philosophical depth of Kafka and Dostoevsky and the unfiltered emotional honesty of Jaun Elia, I prefer writing that makes a person pause and think rather than simply read and move on.

For me, science and writing are not two separate worlds. One has taught me to value accuracy; the other has taught me to understand depth. One deals with what can be measured, while the other explores what cannot always be explained. Somewhere between the two, I have found my own way of seeing the world.

— Robotic Rooh
